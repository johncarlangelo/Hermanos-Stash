Review a feature as a real user would.

Focus on:
- can the user understand what to do immediately?
- can they recover from errors?
- are destructive or irreversible actions clear?
- are file inputs understandable?
- are results obvious?
- is navigation predictable?
- are search/filter/favorite flows efficient?

Do not redesign the product from scratch. Recommend changes that strengthen the established direction.
